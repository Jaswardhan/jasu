The Challenge

Why Metro360?

Verification handled through physical forms, manual processes and office visits leads to systemic inefficiencies.

📋

Paper-Based Processes

Complex paper applications and mandatory physical office visits create delays and inefficiency.

⏰

Missed Deadlines

No automated tracking leads to missed re-verification deadlines and compliance failures.

🔀

Duplicate Records

Inconsistent business/entity information causes duplicate records across isolated systems.

🔍

No Consumer Verification

Consumers lack an easy way to verify instrument authenticity or track complaints.
