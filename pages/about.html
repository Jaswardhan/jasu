Legal Framework & Standards Compliance


Legal Metrology Act, 2009 — Section 24

Establishes the mandatory legal requirement for periodic verification and stamping of all weighing and measuring instruments used in trade, commerce, or public health.

Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011

Defines technical specifications, Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) limits, fee schedules, and test procedures for electronic scales, fuel dispensers, weighbridges, and flow meters.

GATC Rules 2013 & 2026 Amendments

Governs Government Approved Test Centres, enabling accredited private test labs and licensed repairers to conduct digital calibration under LMO oversight.

SIH 26035 / 26036 Integration

Digitizes test report generation, automated rule-based compliance calculation engines, and unified instrument lifecycle tracking.
